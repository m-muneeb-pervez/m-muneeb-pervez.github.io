ICLR 2027

More Agents, Same Exposure: Measuring the Safety of Tool-Partitioned Agent Teams

Ilies Benhabbour, Muhammad Muneeb Pervez, Zafar Ayyub Qazi, Marco Canini

Introduces safeMAS, a framework for measuring how safe multi-agent LLM systems are against attacks

Key findingSplitting tools across an orchestrated team does not make it safer. A tampered message between agents succeeds in 76% of runs, more than any other attack vector.

Details
  • A measurement framework with 990 scenarios across 4 simulated domains and 240 tools, run on three models
  • With attacks on the channels between agents included, 53% of attacks on orchestrated teams succeed
  • Orchestrated teams make 16 to 60 times as many model calls as a single agent
